Data/Autorater/, Data/Autorater_BT/  (Arena human + autorater votes)
    Collected on the Arena platform and released here with permission.  Thirty
    models that do not appear in any figure of the paper carry pseudonyms of the
    form "model-NN"; their release dates are blanked in model_release.json for
    the same reason.  The remaining models keep their public names.  No mapping
    between pseudonyms and real names is distributed with this deposit.

Data/Pew/  (American Trends Panel)
    Contains NO respondent-level ATP data.  Each (item, wave, party, region)
    cell is reduced to six scalars -- the weighted gold approval rate, the
    weighted gap and its standard error, the weighted synthetic mean and its
    standard error, and the respondent count.  These are the only functionals
    the algorithms evaluate, so the reduction is exact rather than approximate.
    The synthetic (GPT-4o) responses summarised in those scalars were generated
    for this study and are CC BY 4.0.  To rebuild the file from primary sources,
    obtain the ATP waves from the Pew Research Center under their data use
    agreement and run provenance/pew_generation/ followed by
    Alg/data_ingestion/pew_cells.py.

Data/ANES/  (ANES feeling thermometers + GPT-3.5 silicon samples)
    Derived from the replication package of Bisbee et al. (2024), deposited in
    the Harvard Dataverse under a CC0 1.0 public domain waiver,
    https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/VPN481.  Redistributed here under those terms.
    The underlying ANES surveys are available from https://electionstudies.org/.

Data/simulated/
    Fully synthetic, generated by Alg/data_ingestion/simulate.py.  CC0.
